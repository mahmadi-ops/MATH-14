    The curve \(C\) is the intersection of the cylinder \(x^2 + y^2 = 1\) and the surface \(z = 4x^2\text{.}\) Parametrize \(C\) by filling in the three components below, using the standard parametrization of the unit circle for the shadow of \(C\) in the \(xy\)-plane.

    \(x(t) =\)

    \(y(t) =\)
    🔗
    \(z(t) =\)
    🔗
    The parameter \(t\) runs from \(0\) to , which traces \(C\) exactly once.
    🔗
    Solution.
    The shadow of \(C\) in the \(xy\)-plane is the unit circle, so take \(x=\cos(t)\text{,}\) \(y=\sin(t)\) with \(0 \le t \le 2\pi \approx 6.283\text{.}\) Substituting into \(z=4x^2\) gives \(z = 4\cos^2(t)\text{,}\) so
    \begin{equation*} \mathbf{r}(t)=\left\langle \cos(t),\ \sin(t),\ 4\cos^2(t) \right\rangle, \qquad 0 \le t \le 2\pi . \end{equation*}

    Consider the curve
    🔗
    \begin{equation*} \mathbf{r}(t) = \left\langle \ln(t),\, \frac{t-1}{t+2},\, t\ln(t) \right\rangle \end{equation*}
    and the point \(P(0,0,0)\) on it.
    🔗
    The parameter value at \(P\) is \(t_0 =\) .
    🔗
    The tangent line at \(P\) has direction vector \(\mathbf{r}\,'(t_0) = \langle\) , , \(\rangle\text{.}\)
    🔗
    Solution.
    The point \(P\) is reached when \(\ln(t)=0\text{,}\) that is, at \(t_0=1\text{;}\) the other two components vanish there as well. Differentiating,
    \begin{equation*} \mathbf{r}\,'(t) = \left\langle \frac{1}{t},\ \frac{3}{(t+2)^2},\ \ln(t)+1 \right\rangle , \end{equation*}

    so \(\mathbf{r}\,'(1)=\left\langle 1,\ \tfrac13,\ 1\right\rangle\) and the tangent line is
    \begin{equation*} \left\langle x,y,z\right\rangle = s\left\langle 1,\ \tfrac13,\ 1 \right\rangle , \qquad s \in \R . \end{equation*}

    4. Where Is the Object After Travelling 15?

    The path of an object is
    🔗
    \begin{equation*} \mathbf{r}(t) = \left\langle 12t,\, 8t^{3/2},\, 3t^2 \right\rangle , \qquad t \ge 0, \end{equation*}
    starting at the origin. Find where it is after travelling a distance of \(15\text{.}\)
    🔗
    Its speed at \(t=0\) is .
    🔗
    The object has travelled a distance of \(15\) at time \(t =\) .
    🔗
    At that moment it is at the point \(\langle\) , , \(\rangle\text{.}\)
    🔗
    Solution.
    Differentiating, \(\mathbf{v}(t)=\left\langle 12,\ 12t^{1/2},\ 6t\right\rangle\text{,}\) and the speed collapses to a perfect square:
    \begin{equation*} \left\lVert\mathbf{v}(t)\right\rVert = \sqrt{144+144t+36t^2} = \sqrt{36(t+2)^2} = 6(t+2). \end{equation*}
    🔗
    Hence the distance travelled by time \(t\) is
    \begin{equation*} s(t)=\int_0^{t} 6(\tau+2)\,d\tau = 3t^2+12t , \end{equation*}
    🔗
    and \(3t^2+12t=15\) gives \(t^2+4t-5=(t+5)(t-1)=0\text{,}\) so \(t=1\text{.}\) The object is then at \(\mathbf{r}(1)=\left\langle 12,\ 8,\ 3\right\rangle\text{.}\)
