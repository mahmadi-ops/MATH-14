Checkpoint 1.5.19. A Line Integral Along a Segment.

Compute the line integral of the vector field along the line segment shown in Figure 1.5.20.
🔗
Answer.
\(\displaystyle \int_C \mathbf{F}\cdot d\mathbf{r} = 1 + \frac{4\sqrt2}{3} \approx 2.886 .\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
The field \(\mathbf{F}=\tfrac13\left\langle z,\ z,\ x+y\right\rangle\) is conservative: it is smooth on all of \(\R^3\) and
🔗
\begin{align*} \frac{\partial}{\partial y}\left(\frac{z}{3}\right) \amp = 0 = \frac{\partial}{\partial x}\left(\frac{z}{3}\right),\\ \frac{\partial}{\partial z}\left(\frac{z}{3}\right) \amp = \frac13 = \frac{\partial}{\partial x}\left(\frac{x+y}{3}\right) = \frac{\partial}{\partial y}\left(\frac{x+y}{3}\right). \end{align*}
A potential is found by inspection:
🔗
\begin{equation} f(x,y,z) = \frac{(x+y)z}{3}, \qquad \nabla f = \left\langle \frac{z}{3},\ \frac{z}{3},\ \frac{x+y}{3}\right\rangle = \mathbf{F} .\tag{1.5.9} \end{equation}
So there is no need to parametrize the segment at all. By (1.5.3), with \(A=(0,1,-1)\) and \(B=(1,2\sqrt2,2)\text{,}\)
🔗
\begin{align*} \int_C \mathbf{F}\cdot d\mathbf{r} \amp = f(B)-f(A) = \frac{\left(1+2\sqrt2\right)(2)}{3} - \frac{(0+1)(-1)}{3}\\ \amp = \frac{2+4\sqrt2}{3} + \frac13 = 1 + \frac{4\sqrt2}{3} \approx 2.886 . \end{align*}
