Example 1.3.12. Reading the wall off the picture.

Calculate the area of the wall marked with the vertical dashed blue lines in Figure 1.3.13.
🔗
Solution.
The marked wall stands on the quarter of the circle \(x^2+y^2=1\) in the first quadrant, from \((1,0)\) to \((0,1)\text{,}\) and its height is \(f(x,y) = y e^x\text{.}\) By (1.3.18) its area is \(\int_C y e^x\,ds\text{.}\) We begin by parametrizing the base of the wall:
\begin{align*} \mathbf{r}(t) \amp = \left\langle \cos t,\ \sin t \right\rangle , \qquad 0 \le t \le \frac{\pi}{2} ,\\ \mathbf{r}'(t) \amp = \left\langle -\sin t,\ \cos t \right\rangle ,\\ \left\lVert \mathbf{r}'(t) \right\rVert \amp = \sqrt{\sin^2 t + \cos^2 t} = 1 , \end{align*}
🔗
so \(ds = dt\text{,}\) and hence for the area we have
\begin{align*} \int_C y e^x\,ds \amp = \int_0^{\pi/2} \sin t\; e^{\cos t}\,dt\\ \amp = -e^{\cos t}\Big|_0^{\pi/2} = -e^0 + e^1 = e - 1 . \end{align*}
