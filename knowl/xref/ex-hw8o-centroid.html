Exercise 1. The Centroid of Cone Plus Cylinder.

For the solid of Figure 5.8.1 with constant density \(\delta\text{:}\) the mass of the cylinder is \(m_1 = \) times \(\delta\text{,}\) and the mass of the cone is \(m_2 = \) times \(\delta\text{.}\)
🔗
The centroid lies on the \(z\)-axis at \(z = \) .
🔗
Solution.
By symmetry the cylinder’s own centroid is the origin, so only the cone moves the answer. The cylinder has volume \(\pi(1)^2(2) = 2\pi\) and the cone
🔗
\begin{equation*} m_2 = \delta\iint_R\left(1-\sqrt{x^2+y^2}\right) dA = \delta\left(\tfrac12 - \tfrac13\right)(2\pi) = \frac{\pi\delta}{3} . \end{equation*}
The cone’s own centroid sits at \(z = 1 + \frac34 = \frac74\text{,}\) so with \(M = 2\pi\delta + \frac{\pi\delta}{3} = \frac{7\pi\delta}{3}\text{,}\)
🔗
\begin{equation*} \overline{z} = \frac{m_2}{M}\cdot\frac74 = \frac{\pi\delta/3}{7\pi\delta/3}\cdot\frac74 = \frac14 , \end{equation*}
so the centroid is \(\left\langle 0, 0, \frac14 \right\rangle\text{.}\)
