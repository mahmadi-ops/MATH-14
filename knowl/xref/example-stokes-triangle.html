Example 3.2.16. Around a Triangle, Twice.

Evaluate \(\oint_C \mathbf{F} \cdot d\mathbf{r}\) for
\begin{equation*} \mathbf{F} = (y - z)\,\mathbf{i} + (x + z)\,\mathbf{j} + \left(x^2 + y\right)\mathbf{k}, \end{equation*}
where \(C\) goes from \((2,0,0)\) to \((0,2,0)\) to \((0,0,4)\) and back to \((2,0,0)\) along straight line segments, as in Figure 3.2.17. Do the computation twice: directly, and by Stokes’ theorem.
