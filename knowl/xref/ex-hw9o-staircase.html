Solution.
Closing the path with the segment \(C_3\) down the \(y\)-axis gives a simple closed curve, and \(\frac{\partial N}{\partial x} - \frac{\partial M}{\partial y} = 1-(-1) = 2\text{,}\) so by Green’s theorem, (3.1.5), the closed loop integral is \(2 \times 55 = 110\text{,}\) where \(55 = 50 + 10\left(\frac12\right)\) is the enclosed area.
