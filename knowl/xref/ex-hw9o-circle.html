Exercise 2. A Fierce-Looking Integral Around a Circle.

For the integral of Exercise 2 around the unit circle: after simplification \(\frac{\partial N}{\partial x} - \frac{\partial M}{\partial y}\) equals (in terms of \(y\)), and the integral equals .
🔗
Solution.
Differentiating, \(\frac{\partial M}{\partial y} = \frac{y}{\sqrt{x^2+y^2}}\) and \(\frac{\partial N}{\partial x} = y\left(y + \frac{1}{\sqrt{x^2+y^2}}\right)\text{,}\) so their difference is \(y^2\text{.}\) By Green’s theorem, (3.1.5),
🔗
\begin{equation*} \oint_C = \iint_R y^2\,dx\,dy = \int_0^{2\pi}\!\!\int_0^1 r^3\sin^2\theta\,dr\,d\theta = \frac{\pi}{4} . \end{equation*}
