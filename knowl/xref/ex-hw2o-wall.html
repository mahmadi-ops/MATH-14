Exercise 4. Area of a Curved Wall.

Find the surface area of the wall of Figure 5.2.2, whose height is \(f(x,y) = x^2-y^2+3\) over the unit circle.
🔗
The surface area of the wall is .
🔗
Solution.
With \(\mathbf{r}(t) = \left\langle \cos t, \sin t \right\rangle\) the speed is \(1\) and
🔗
\begin{equation*} f = \cos^2 t - \sin^2 t + 3 = \cos(2t) + 3 , \end{equation*}
so the area is
🔗
\begin{equation*} \int_0^{2\pi} \left(\cos(2t)+3\right) dt = 6\pi \approx 18.850 . \end{equation*}
