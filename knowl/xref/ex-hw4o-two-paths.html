Exercise 1. Which Path Takes Less Work?

For the field of Figure 5.4.1: over which path does \(\mathbf{F}\) perform less work? Type ADC or ABC.
🔗
Answer:
🔗
Is \(\mathbf{F}\) conservative? Type yes or no.
🔗
Answer:
🔗
Solution.
Both paths run along two sides of the square. The horizontal sides \(AD\) and \(BC\) contribute the same, but the vertical side \(DC\) sits where the field is longer than it is along \(AB\text{,}\) and the angle between \(\mathbf{F}\) and \(\hat{\mathbf{T}}\) is the same on both. Hence \(\int_{ADC} \gt \int_{ABC}\text{,}\) so \(ABC\) is the path of less work.
🔗
Because the two paths share their endpoints and give different work, \(\mathbf{F}\) is not conservative: a conservative field is path-independent.
