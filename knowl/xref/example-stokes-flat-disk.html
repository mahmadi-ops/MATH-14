Example 3.2.12. A Term That Cannot Be Integrated.

Evaluate \(\oint_C \mathbf{F} \cdot d\mathbf{r}\) for
\begin{equation*} \mathbf{F} = \left[\cos\left(x^{100}\right) + 3y\right]\mathbf{i} + 4z\,\mathbf{j} + 2y\,\mathbf{k}, \end{equation*}
where \(C\) is the circle \(x^2 + y^2 = 9\text{,}\) \(z = 2\text{,}\) traversed counterclockwise when seen from above.
