Exercise 1. The Sign of a Line Integral.

For the field and curve of Figure 5.3.1, is \(\displaystyle\oint_C \mathbf{F} \cdot d\mathbf{r}\) positive, negative, or zero? Type one word: positive, negative, or zero.
🔗
Answer:
🔗
Solution.
Along the bottom of \(C\) the field and the unit tangent make acute angles, so \(\mathbf{F} \cdot \hat{\mathbf{T}} \gt 0\) there; along the top they make obtuse angles, so \(\mathbf{F} \cdot \hat{\mathbf{T}} \lt 0\text{.}\) The field is longer—stronger—over the top half, so the negative contribution wins and the integral is negative.
